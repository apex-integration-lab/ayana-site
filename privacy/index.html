Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Ayana is a private space for approved family members to share books, photos, and milestones. This policy explains the information needed to provide that space and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

  • Account and access: Your email address, phone number if previously supplied for phone sign-in, display name, sign-in credentials, and your membership in a family space. Authentication providers may process information needed to sign you in.
  • Family content: Books and reading details; photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, songs, and post dates; journal and shared notes; monthly answers, drafts and PDF exports; and information entered in My Info, including milestones, birth dates, and growth measurements. Family reports may also contain imported sleep, feeding and bath summaries. Optional monthly prompts can ask about heritage, birth and adoption history. This content may include information about children that an approved adult chooses to add.
  • Location you choose to share: When you add a place to a post, Ayana stores the place label shown in the composer. You may enter it yourself or let the app suggest it from device location or a photo's location metadata. The device geocoding provider may process coordinates to suggest a place. If it cannot resolve a place name, the suggested label may contain coordinates rounded to three decimal places, which can still identify a precise location. Review or remove the label before posting.
  • Embedded media details: Ayana renders some photos before upload, but videos and older retained originals may still contain embedded capture dates, precise location, or other device metadata. Do not assume removing the visible place label removes metadata inside a file.
  • Support messages: Information you send to our support address so we can respond to your request.

Information generated while using Ayana

Ayana stores account and content activity needed for features such as feed reactions and notifications. If you enable push notifications, it stores a device push token and your notification preferences. When error monitoring is configured, Ayana sends crash, performance and other diagnostics to Sentry to help us fix problems. These can include operation timings, error types, app and device information, session or trace identifiers, and a separate diagnostic identifier linked to your Ayana account through a private mapping. This identifier does not contain your email or name, but it is not anonymous.

Sentry can receive a masked screen replay around an error. Text, images, videos and camera surfaces are configured to be masked; routine session replay is disabled. Ayana also sends counts of whether cover-crop and caption suggestions were shown, used, changed or dismissed to understand how these features work for members. These feature-outcome counts do not include your photos or caption text. Diagnostics and replay are subject to the service provider’s processing and retention.

Ayana records when a member taps Download on a family feed post, including which photos or videos were saved or failed to save. On supported devices, Ayana also records a screenshot signal while the family feed or a photo viewer is visible. The signal does not include the screenshot image and may not identify a post. Photos saved through Ayana may contain a hidden identifier linked to that individual download; editing a photo may remove it. Only administrators of the same family space can view these activity records or check a photo for a matching identifier. These records and identifiers are scheduled for cleanup after 90 days, including when a member has left the space. Android may display a system notice when a screenshot signal is detected.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to authenticate members, provide the family library and feed, deliver requested notifications, respond to support, maintain app reliability, and understand use of cover-crop and caption suggestions. Family content is available to approved members of its family space; members with administrative access can manage that space. Journal entries are restricted to family administrators, not just the original author; shared notes and monthly answers follow their sharing settings. Content is not intended to be public.

Supabase hosts Ayana's accounts, database, and private media storage. Where legacy phone authentication is enabled, Bird processes the phone number and one-time code for sign-in message delivery; the current app offers email, Apple and Google sign-in. Google Books and Open Library receive book searches or ISBNs you submit for lookup, including title or author text extracted from a cover on your device; their servers also receive requests for cover images. These providers and device geocoding services may process network information associated with requests. If push notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging and the device platform's push service process delivery information. Sentry processes diagnostics when configured. We do not use Ayana content for advertising or sell it to advertisers.

On supported Apple devices, optional caption ideas are generated on the device. Photos are not sent to a caption service for that feature.

Content review, reports, and blocking

New posts, comments, photos and videos, member book notes, My Info entries, display names, and edits require approval before other members can see them. Family administrators can review and publish their own submissions. Pending revisions are available to their author and authorized family administrators for review; an existing approved version remains visible until an edit is approved.

We store submissions, review decisions, accepted family rules, abuse reports, member blocks, and family access restrictions to operate these protections. Reports include your account, the reported member or content, your selected reason, any explanation you provide, and a captured copy of relevant content. Family access bans are separate from personal blocks. Authorized family administrators can review reported and pending content even when a personal block applies. For concerns involving an administrator, contact support@aaron.software.

A personal block hides the two members' personal content from each other and stops their interactions and notifications. Shared family records remain available, with blocked members' embedded contributions hidden. Blocking cannot recall files someone has already downloaded or shared, or immediately invalidate a media link that has not yet expired.

Resolved abuse reports are scheduled for cleanup 90 days after resolution. Rejected, withdrawn, or replaced unpublished submission content is scheduled for cleanup 90 days after that status change. Open reports, pending submissions, and current published content do not expire under this rule. When published content is replaced or deleted, its obsolete stored review copy is cleared immediately; a report may retain its separate captured evidence under the report rule.

Submission and revision identifiers, account references, decisions and timestamps remain to prevent duplicate or stale changes until account cleanup. Media removal runs separately and retries as needed; files still referenced by current content, retained reports or other retained records remain until that use ends.

For Sign in with Apple, Ayana exchanges authorization credentials on the server and stores an encrypted credential so it can revoke authorization when you delete your account. These credentials are not shared with family members.

Storage and protection

Ayana limits access to family data by account and family space. Media is kept in private storage and shared with approved members through time-limited links. No online service can promise absolute security; contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Your choices and requests

You can choose what to add, edit or remove supported content in the app, and turn off optional location and notification permissions in your device settings. To delete your account and associated information, follow the account deletion instructions. For access or correction requests, email support@aaron.software. We may need to verify your identity for requests made outside the signed-in app.

Account deletion removes associated content, including attributable monthly copies. Shared monthly drafts and PDF exports with missing or incomplete contributor attribution, including new copies incorporating older content, will be removed from family spaces you belong to or previously contributed to, including for other members. This removal of derived copies preserves other members’ unrelated original posts, notes, photos and books. A shared note or journal entry containing your last edit may be removed as a whole. Previously downloaded files, sent messages and recipient copies cannot be recalled. The ordinary 90-day moderation schedule does not delay account deletion.

Deleting the account also removes its private diagnostic-ID mapping; this does not automatically erase diagnostics already sent to Sentry. Contact us for help with previously transmitted diagnostic data.

Children and family content

Ayana is intended for approved adult members of a family space, not for children to create their own accounts. Adults who add information about children should consider the sensitivity of health, growth, birth and family-history details and share only what is appropriate for the members who can access them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Ayana changes. The effective date above shows when this version took effect. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@aaron.software.
