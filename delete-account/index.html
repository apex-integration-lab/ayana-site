Ayana account help

Delete your Ayana account

Effective October 31, 2026

Open Ayana, go to Profile → Delete account, and confirm. You do not need to email support to delete your account in the app.

Before you confirm

Deletion permanently removes your account and associated content, including posts, notes, books, My Info entries, and uploaded photos or videos. Shared monthly drafts and PDF exports containing your contributions are also removed.

Older shared monthly drafts and PDF exports whose contributors cannot be identified will also be removed from affected family spaces, including for other members. Removing these derived copies preserves other members’ unrelated original posts, notes, photos and books. A shared note or journal entry containing your last edit may be removed as a whole because your contribution cannot be separated from its text. Unrelated, fully attributed monthly documents remain.

This cannot be undone. Ayana cannot recall files that someone has already downloaded, messages already sent, or copies held by recipients. Deleting your account is different from leaving a space or blocking a member.

If you used Sign in with Apple

Ayana revokes saved Apple authorizations before completing account deletion. If an older account has no saved authorization for its current Apple connection, the app offers Connect Apple or Delete without connecting Apple. You can choose the latter to delete your Ayana data and then follow the displayed instructions to remove Ayana from Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings. See Apple’s instructions for managing Sign in with Apple.

If revoking a saved Apple authorization fails, deletion does not report success; retry when the problem is resolved. The manual option does not bypass a saved authorization. If cleanup is interrupted or needs another attempt, follow the app’s retry message. Do not assume deletion is complete until the app confirms it.

What happens to your data

Account deletion removes associated records and media from the active Ayana service and removes the sign-in account. The ordinary 90-day schedule for resolved reports and rejected or withdrawn unpublished submissions does not postpone account deletion. Previously transmitted diagnostic events are handled separately by the diagnostic provider; deleting the account’s private diagnostic-ID mapping does not automatically erase those events. Read our privacy policy or contact us for help with diagnostic data.

Optional email help if you cannot sign in

Email us from the address on your Ayana account if you can. Include the account email address and say that you want help signing in or want your account and associated data deleted. We will verify that the account is yours before completing an email request. Never send your password or sign-in code.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to support@aaron.software with the subject “Ayana account and data deletion request.”
