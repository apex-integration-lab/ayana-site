Ayana account help

Delete your Ayana account

You can delete your account and associated data in the app, or send us a request here if you cannot sign in.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the address on your Ayana account if you can. Include the account email address and say that you want your Ayana account and associated data deleted. We will verify that the account is yours before completing the request.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to support@aaron.software with the subject “Ayana account and data deletion request.” You can request deletion even if you cannot sign in. Never send your password or sign-in code.

Delete your account in Ayana

Open Ayana, go to Profile → Delete account, and confirm. This deletes your account and the content you created, including your books, feed posts, photos and videos, song suggestions, and My Info entries.

What happens to your data

Deleting your account removes your account and associated content from the active service. Content another family member created remains in their account. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is required for security or legal reasons. Read our privacy policy for more information.
