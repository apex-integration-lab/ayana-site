Ayana support

Questions about your account, your family space, or something that isn't working? Email us and we'll help you sort it out.

Contact us

support@aaron.software

Tell us what happened, which device you're using, and the email address on your Ayana account if it's relevant. Please don't send passwords, sign-in codes, or sensitive family photos by email.

Common requests

  • Can't sign in? Use the same Apple or Google account you used for Ayana. Apple Hide My Email is supported. Contact us if sign-in does not finish or you cannot access your existing family space.
  • Need an invitation? Ask your family's administrator for a personal invitation code or a space join link. A space join request needs administrator approval.
  • Book search or barcode issue? Include the book title or ISBN and tell us whether you searched or scanned it. You can enter a book manually when a lookup does not find it.
  • Photo or feed problem? Tell us whether you're adding, viewing, or editing a post, and include any error message you see. Avoid sending family photos by email unless we ask for one.
  • Want to change data? You can manage supported posts and details in the app. Email us for account access or correction requests.
  • Found a problem? Include the steps you took and any error message, without sharing private account credentials.

Report abuse or a safety concern

Use Report on content or a member in Ayana to ask your family's administrators to review it. You can also block a member to stop personal content and interactions in both directions. Administrators handle publication review and can separately remove or ban members from their family space.

If a report concerns an administrator, remains unresolved, or you need help outside your family space, email support@aaron.software. Aaron handles these escalations and aims to respond within 24 hours. Include a brief description and the relevant account or family space, without sending passwords, sign-in codes, or sensitive family photos.

Request account deletion

You can delete your account in Ayana or request deletion by email even if you cannot sign in. See how to delete your account and associated data.
